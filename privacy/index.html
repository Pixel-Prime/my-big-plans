Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

My Big Plans is developed by Andy Copsey. It is a personal savings tracker and does not require a separate app account.

Your savings records

The app stores information you enter, including plan names, descriptions, savings targets, dates, amounts, transaction notes and reminder preferences, on your device to provide its tracking and planning features. If you choose a personal image for a plan, it forms part of your plan data.

The app does not connect to your bank, hold funds or initiate payments. Adding or withdrawing money in the app only changes your records.

iCloud

When iCloud is available, the app uses Apple's private CloudKit storage to synchronise your app data across devices signed into your iCloud account. We do not operate a separate server for your savings records. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in its privacy policy.

Reminders

Optional savings reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can change them in the app or disable notification permission in iOS Settings. Reminder notifications may display a plan name on your lock screen, depending on your notification preview settings.

Analytics and advertising

My Big Plans does not include third-party analytics or advertising trackers and does not sell your personal information.

Managing your data

You can edit or delete individual plans and transactions within the app. Deletions may synchronise to your other devices when iCloud is available. Removing the app from a device does not necessarily delete data stored in iCloud. You can manage iCloud storage through your Apple account settings.

This website and support

This static website does not set its own cookies or use analytics scripts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the service. See GitHub's privacy statement. If you email us, we use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry and resolve issues. Please avoid sending sensitive financial information.
