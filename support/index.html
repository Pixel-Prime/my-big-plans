Frequently asked questions

Does the app actually move money?

No. My Big Plans only records the amounts you enter. Make real deposits and withdrawals separately using your usual savings arrangements.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes. Your plans are stored on your device. iCloud changes can sync when connectivity returns.

Why aren't my plans appearing on another device?

Check that both devices use the same Apple account, iCloud is enabled for the app and both have an internet connection. Allow time for syncing. Avoid deleting or reinstalling the app while troubleshooting missing data.

Why haven't I received a reminder?

Check the plan's reminder settings and notification permission in iOS Settings. Reminders pause when a plan is fully funded or inactive. Focus modes and notification settings may affect delivery.

Can I share a plan with someone else?

My Big Plans is currently designed for personal saving. Collaborative plans are not supported.
